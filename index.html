Who we are

A Chartered Accountancy firm built on trust and technical depth

Established on a legacy dating back to 2001, VEGHA & ASSOCIATES brings together accomplished Chartered Accountants with complementary expertise across assurance, taxation, corporate advisory, risk management and regulatory compliance. Decades of collective experience, combined with contemporary technology-driven practice, let us deliver services that are practical, reliable and commercially relevant.

Partner-led, always

Every engagement is led by a partner. You get experienced judgement, timely communication and senior attention, not a handoff to a junior team.

Multidisciplinary by design

Our partners collaborate rather than operate as individual practitioners, so a tax question gets an answer informed by audit, regulatory and advisory perspectives.

Commercially grounded

Technically correct is the starting point, not the finish line. Our advice accounts for how your business actually operates and what it is trying to achieve.
