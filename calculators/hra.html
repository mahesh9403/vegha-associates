What this calculator computes

House rent allowance is partly exempt from tax under section 10(13A) if you actually pay rent for accommodation you occupy. This calculator applies the statutory test to your figures. You enter your basic salary plus dearness allowance forming part of retirement benefits, the HRA received, the rent you paid and whether the city is a metro. It returns the exempt amount, the taxable balance and the workings for all three limbs of the test.

The three way minimum rule

The exemption is the least of three amounts, computed for the period the conditions were satisfied.

  • The actual house rent allowance received from your employer.
  • Rent actually paid, reduced by ten percent of salary.
  • Fifty percent of salary if the accommodation is in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata or Chennai, and forty percent of salary anywhere else.

Exempt HRA = least of (HRA received) or (Rent paid - 10 percent of salary) or (50 percent of salary for metro, else 40 percent of salary)

For this purpose salary means basic pay plus dearness allowance where the terms of employment so provide, plus commission based on a fixed percentage of turnover. It does not include other allowances or perquisites.

How to read the results

  • If your rent is low relative to your salary, the second limb usually binds, and a large part of your HRA stays taxable. If your rent is high, the first or third limb binds instead.
  • Where salary, HRA or rent changed during the year, the calculation must be done separately for each period and the results added. A single annual computation will be wrong.
  • The exempt portion does not appear as income at all. The balance is added to your salary income and taxed at your applicable rate.

Practical points for salaried taxpayers

  • The HRA exemption is available only if you are taxed under the old regime. Taxpayers opting for the new regime cannot claim it, so run the comparison before choosing.
  • Keep rent receipts and the rent agreement. Where rent paid in the year exceeds one lakh rupees, you must report the landlord permanent account number to your employer. Tax may also need to be deducted at source on rent above the prescribed monthly threshold, which is the tenant obligation, not the landlord.
  • You can claim HRA and a housing loan deduction in the same year if the facts support it, for example where you own a house in one city and genuinely live on rent in another. Pay rent by bank transfer and keep the trail clean, since rent claimed to a family member draws close scrutiny.

Answers

Frequently asked questions

Can I claim HRA exemption under the new tax regime?

No. The exemption under section 10(13A) is one of the deductions and exemptions given up when you opt for the new regime. Salaried taxpayers with high rent and a meaningful HRA component often find the old regime works out better for this reason. Compare both regimes before your employer locks in your declaration for the year.

Which cities count as metro for HRA purposes?

Only Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai qualify for the fifty percent limb. Every other city, including Hyderabad, Bengaluru and Pune, is treated as non metro and attracts the forty percent limb, regardless of how expensive housing there has become. The classification follows the Income Tax Act and not the local cost of living.

Can I pay rent to my parents and claim HRA?

Yes, provided the arrangement is genuine. The property must actually be owned by the parent, you must genuinely occupy it, rent must be paid by traceable bank transfer, and the parent must declare that rent as income from house property in their own return. Departments do examine these claims, so a rent agreement and consistent bank records are essential.

What if I forgot to submit rent receipts to my employer?

You can still claim the exemption when filing your return, even if your employer did not consider it in Form 16 and deducted more tax. Compute the exempt amount correctly, claim it in the return and the excess tax deducted comes back as a refund. Retain the receipts, agreement and payment proof, since the claim may be queried later.
