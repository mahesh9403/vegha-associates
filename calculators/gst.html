What this calculator computes

Goods and Services Tax is charged as a percentage of the taxable value of a supply. This calculator works in both directions. In add GST mode you enter the base price and the applicable rate, and it returns the tax amount and the final invoice value. In remove GST mode you enter an amount that already includes tax, and it works backwards to the taxable value and the tax embedded in it. Both modes show the split between the central and state components.

The formula in words

Adding GST is straightforward: apply the rate to the taxable value. Removing GST is where most errors happen, because you cannot simply take the rate off the gross figure. You must divide the inclusive amount by one plus the rate.

GST amount = Taxable value x (Rate / 100)

Taxable value = Inclusive amount / (1 + (Rate / 100))

For example, applying eighteen percent to a base of one hundred gives an invoice of one hundred and eighteen. Going the other way, an inclusive amount is divided by 1.18 to recover the base.

How the tax splits

  • For a supply within the same state or union territory, the tax is levied half as CGST and half as SGST or UTGST. The total burden on the customer is unchanged, only the allocation between the centre and the state differs.
  • For a supply from one state to another, and for imports, a single IGST is charged at the full rate.
  • The rate structure is a small set of slabs notified by the GST Council, together with nil rated and exempt categories, a special rate for certain goods such as precious metals and a higher rate with cess on demerit and luxury items. The Council revises rates and slabs from time to time, so confirm the current notification for your HSN or SAC classification before invoicing.

Practical points for Indian businesses

  • Classification decides the rate. Get the HSN code for goods or the SAC for services right, because an incorrect classification can lead to short payment, interest and penalty even where the intent was honest.
  • Registered buyers recover GST as input tax credit, so for business to business supplies the tax is a cash flow item rather than a cost. For business to consumer supplies it is a genuine cost to the customer, which is why MRP based pricing needs the remove GST calculation.
  • Composition scheme dealers, exporters under letter of undertaking and supplies attracting reverse charge follow different mechanics. This calculator handles the standard forward charge case.

Answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Divide the inclusive amount by one plus the rate expressed as a decimal, then subtract the result from the inclusive amount to get the tax. Do not simply apply the rate to the gross figure, as that overstates the tax and understates the taxable value. This matters whenever you price at MRP and need to report the correct taxable value on the invoice.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

They are three collection routes for the same total tax. An intra state supply attracts CGST and SGST in equal halves, going to the centre and the state respectively. An inter state supply, and an import, attracts IGST at the full rate, which the centre later apportions with the destination state. The customer pays the same amount in either case.

Which GST rate applies to my product or service?

The rate depends on the classification of the supply under the HSN code for goods or the SAC for services, as notified by the GST Council. Rates and the slab structure are revised periodically, so the current notification is the only reliable source. If the classification is genuinely ambiguous, an advance ruling or a written opinion is cheaper than a dispute later.

Do I have to register for GST?

Registration is compulsory once your aggregate turnover crosses the threshold notified for your state and type of supply, and it is compulsory regardless of turnover in specified cases such as inter state supply of goods, e commerce operators and persons liable under reverse charge. Voluntary registration is also permitted and is often sensible if your customers are registered businesses who want input tax credit. Ask us to assess your position before you cross the threshold.
