Answers

Frequently asked questions

What is RERA compliance support for a real estate developer?

It covers project registration with the authority, maintaining the prescribed separate project account, periodic reporting, and reconciling project-level costs against collections. We support developers through registration and ongoing quarterly obligations.

Why does a manufacturer need inventory verification by a chartered accountant?

Independent physical verification confirms that recorded stock exists and is valued correctly. It is often required by lenders as a condition of working capital facilities, and it commonly surfaces process gaps in receipt, issue and scrap accounting.

What is a concurrent audit in banking?

A concurrent audit is an ongoing examination of a bank branch's transactions carried out alongside the activity itself, rather than after year-end. It covers advances, income recognition and regulatory compliance, and is reported to bank management at defined intervals.
