Answers

Frequently asked questions

Which businesses in India need a statutory audit?

Every company registered under the Companies Act, 2013 requires a statutory audit regardless of turnover. LLPs need one once turnover exceeds Rs. 40 lakh or contribution exceeds Rs. 25 lakh. We handle the full statutory audit cycle for companies and LLPs across Hyderabad and Telangana.

When is a tax audit required under Section 44AB?

A tax audit applies when business turnover exceeds Rs. 1 crore, or Rs. 10 crore where cash receipts and payments are each under 5% of the total. For professionals the threshold is gross receipts above Rs. 50 lakh. We assess applicability and complete the audit and Form 3CD reporting.

Do you handle GST notices and departmental representation?

Yes. We handle GST litigation end to end, replying to notices, preparing submissions, and appearing before departmental authorities and appellate forums on behalf of clients.

Can you act as an outsourced finance function?

Yes. Our Virtual CFO and financial process outsourcing services cover bookkeeping, payroll, MIS reporting, budgeting and cash flow management for businesses that need senior finance capability without a full-time hire.

Do you advise on UAE corporate tax and cross-border structuring?

Yes. Our international practice covers UAE corporate tax advisory, cross-border structuring, FEMA compliance and foreign investment advisory for businesses operating in more than one jurisdiction.
